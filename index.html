03 / SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION

The Fundamental Principles
of M4NA

Mechanics for New Age (M4NA) presents a proposed framework for examining the relationship between applied force, motion, velocity and actual travelling distance.

01

Motion

Motion is examined through the actual traverse of an object, including its motion nature, velocity or rate, and travelling distance across successive operations or segments.

02

Applied Force

Applied force is proposed as the operating cause of an object's motion, producing, rebuilding or maintaining its motion value and motion quantity, and operating the object in a specific direction.

03

Motion Value

Motion value is represented by velocity or rate. M4NA examines the corresponding relationship between force value and velocity.

04

Motion Quantity

Motion quantity is represented by actual travelling distance, accumulated through successive segments and operations.

M4NA — FUNDAMENTAL DEFINITION

Definition of Motion

“Motion is the outcome of an applied force which provides a real picture of the traverse of an object by representing its motion nature, value (velocity/rate) and quantity (travelling distance) within successive intervals of the same operation, different operations, or various segments of the same or different intervals.”

MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE

Core Relationships of M4NA

FORCE–VELOCITYf = kv
FORCE–DISTANCEF = kS
AVERAGE VELOCITYV = S/T

Here, f is force value, F is force quantity, v is instantaneous velocity, V is average velocity, S is total travelling distance, T is total operating time, and k is the proportionality constant.
